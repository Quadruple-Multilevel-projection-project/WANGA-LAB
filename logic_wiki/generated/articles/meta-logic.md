# Metalogic

**Stable ID:** meta-logic

## Definition

Study of formal logical systems themselves, including soundness, completeness, consistency, decidability and expressive power.

## Scope and limitations

A system describing itself is not automatically sound or complete; such properties require definitions and proofs.
