# Future Market Assumptions Ledger

## Operating premise

For forward research, maintain a **working scenario** that the agentic/AI automation layer becomes a dominant portion of the addressable software-and-knowledge market over the next five years.

This is a planning assumption, not a forecast or verified market fact.

## What must be collected

Each research cycle should capture organizations, products, papers, standards, registries, and implementations that already operate from one or more of these premises:

1. AI agents are becoming executable units of work.
2. Capabilities are becoming modular, discoverable, reusable artifacts.
3. Registries are becoming infrastructure for discovering and governing agents, tools, and skills.
4. Agents increasingly need dynamic capability discovery and routing.
5. Skills/capabilities require versioning, provenance, evaluation, security, and lifecycle management.
6. Autonomous systems increasingly create, repair, compose, or adapt capabilities.
7. Scientific discovery, engineering, robotics, and software work are all becoming agentic domains.
8. A future capability layer may sit above individual foundation models and runtimes.

## Monthly extraction rule

For every new external observation, create an assumption record containing:

- date;
- source;
- observed organization/system;
- exact observation;
- extracted assumption;
- confidence/status;
- implication for the Logic Mine;
- affected repository/library;
- possible improvement to our architecture;
- evidence link.

Never promote an assumption to a fact without supporting evidence.

## Five-year scenario

**Scenario S5:** by approximately 2031, a large share of economically valuable knowledge-work execution is performed through autonomous or semi-autonomous AI systems.

Use S5 to prioritize research and product preparation.

Do not write “this will happen” as a factual claim. Use “under S5” or “if S5 holds”.

## Current external signals

- Google Cloud Agent Registry now catalogs agents, MCP servers, tools, and standalone skills, with semantic search for reusable skills.
- AWS Agent Registry provides a governed catalog and discovery layer for agents, tools, skills, MCP servers, and custom resources.
- 2026 research describes agentic skills as reusable, executable, portable procedural abstractions with discovery, retrieval/routing, composition, adaptation, evaluation, and security lifecycle.
- Public skill ecosystems are already large, while research identifies substantial quality and reusability defects.

These observations establish an emerging capability/registry layer. They do not prove the full Logic Mine thesis.

## Research question

The central question is:

**Who is already building from the S5 premise, what infrastructure are they creating, what assumptions are embedded in their products, and what remains missing for a continuous Logic Mine that can generate future-ready logic capabilities?**

## Improvement loop

OBSERVATION → ASSUMPTION → EVIDENCE → GAP → ARCHITECTURE CHANGE → TEST → NEW OBSERVATION

This ledger is intended to accumulate the extracted assumptions over time rather than overwrite previous conclusions.


## Valuation horizon model

Valuation is tracked as a scenario model, not as a guaranteed price forecast.

### S5 / 2031 working scenario

Under S5, the addressable market for agentic execution and its supporting capability infrastructure expands materially. Current external forecasts already place agentic AI/platform markets at tens to hundreds of billions of dollars by 2030, depending on scope and definition. These are market forecasts, not forecasts for WANGA-LAB.

### S10 / 2036 extension

S10 extends the S5 scenario by assuming continued migration of economically valuable work toward autonomous systems and increasing demand for reusable capabilities, logic, orchestration, verification, and governance.

S10 is a strategic scenario only.

### WANGA valuation rule

Do not mechanically increase the company's valuation merely because the forecast horizon gets closer.

Instead update four independent variables:

1. Market size evidence — external market estimates and actual spending.
2. Capability scarcity — evidence that the required logic/capability layer is difficult to produce.
3. WANGA technical proof — demonstrated extraction, verification, reuse, and productization.
4. Commercial proof — customers, recurring revenue, licensing, usage, or strategic contracts.

Illustrative valuation bands should therefore be recalculated whenever one of these variables changes.

### Evidence rule

A future market becoming larger does not prove that WANGA captures it.

Use:

MARKET GROWTH ≠ CAPTURE ≠ COMPANY VALUE

Current market evidence: Gartner forecasts agentic AI software spending at $985B by 2030; IDC forecasts agentic AI platforms at $41.5B by 2030. The difference demonstrates why market definitions must be kept explicit.

Sources:
- Gartner, Forecast Analysis: Agentic AI Spending in Software Markets (2026).
- IDC, Worldwide Agentic AI Platforms Forecast, 2026–2030 (2026).

These figures are external market forecasts and should not be presented as WANGA valuation.
