# GBM-001 — Initial Research Brief

**Status:** Assessment in progress  
**Prepared:** 2026-11-15  
**Scope:** Public-source review only

## 1. Verified baseline

### OECD common reporting framework
The OECD publication *Towards a common reporting framework for AI incidents* (28 February 2025) proposes a common framework intended to provide a global benchmark across jurisdictions and sectors. It includes 29 criteria and is designed to support comparable reporting while allowing jurisdictions to adapt responses to domestic policy and legal frameworks.

Source: https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/towards-a-common-reporting-framework-for-ai-incidents_f326d4ac-en.html

### OECD AI Incidents Monitor
The OECD AI incidents overview describes the work to make reporting and monitoring consistent and interoperable globally, and presents the AI Incidents Monitor as an evidence resource for policymakers, practitioners and stakeholders.

Source: https://oecd.ai/en/site/incidents

### Monitor methodology and limitations
The OECD methodology page says the monitor identifies incidents and hazards from reputable international news sources, uses a classification process, enriches records and clusters related coverage. It explicitly notes that publicly reported incidents are only a subset of incidents and hazards worldwide.

Source: https://oecd.ai/en/incidents-methodology

## 2. Initial strategic assessment

The proposed institute should not begin by duplicating the OECD monitor. A more defensible potential contribution is an independent, auditable research layer focused on:
- provenance and traceability from each claim to its public source;
- reconstruction timelines for publicly documented cases;
- comparison of reporting fields and terminology across jurisdictions;
- explicit confidence and uncertainty labels;
- reproducible review and correction history.

This is an **Assessment**, not a verified market gap. GBM-001 must compare existing initiatives and tools before recommending a product or service.

## 3. First 90-day pilot proposal

**Days 1–30 — Landscape and taxonomy**
- Inventory public reporting frameworks, monitors and relevant incident databases.
- Compare scope, definitions, fields, update methods, coverage and limitations.
- Deliver a source register and overlap/gap matrix.

**Days 31–60 — Evidence reconstruction prototype**
- Select up to five already-public, well-documented cases only.
- Build source-linked timelines and claim-level provenance records.
- Record conflicting accounts and missing information without resolving by guesswork.

**Days 61–90 — Validation and operating model**
- Have an independent reviewer assess reproducibility and source quality.
- Publish an internal pilot evaluation, governance rules and go/no-go recommendation.
- Do not launch public submissions or collect sensitive data during this pilot.

## 4. Proposed success criteria

- 100% of material factual claims in pilot outputs link to a source.
- Every case has a documented inclusion rationale and a clear uncertainty log.
- A second reviewer can reproduce each timeline from the cited public sources.
- Existing initiatives and duplicate coverage are explicitly mapped.
- No unsupported claim of endorsement, partnership or regulatory authority.

## 5. Risks and controls

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Media coverage bias | State coverage limits; compare multiple source types where available |
| False or disputed allegations | Use neutral language; preserve conflicting accounts; no unsupported attribution |
| Duplicate effort | Complete ecosystem inventory before building a database |
| Jurisdictional differences | Separate common descriptive fields from local legal classifications |
| Overstated confidence | Claim-level provenance and explicit evidence labels |

## 6. Current decision

**Proceed with the 90-day research pilot design, not with a public incident platform.** The next required work is the full ecosystem inventory and field-by-field comparison. This is a provisional management recommendation, not a verified conclusion about market demand.
