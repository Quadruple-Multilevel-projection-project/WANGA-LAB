## Buyer and problem hypothesis
Target buyer: owner/operator or marketing/operations lead responsible for a small or mid-sized Wix site; secondary channel hypothesis: a Wix agency managing multiple client sites.

Problem hypothesis: site owners may lack a single evidence-linked view of stale page content, missing metadata, navigation/route uncertainty and public claims that are not tied to a source. The cost of the problem and buyer budget authority have not been validated.

## Bounded pilot offer
**Evidence-led Wix Site & Claims Audit**
- One authorized public Wix site.
- Up to 10 static pages plus up to 3 dynamic-route checks.
- Review page titles, meta descriptions, H1s, static internal links, image alt attributes and selected public claims supplied by the buyer.
- Deliver a page inventory, evidence-linked findings, up to 10 prioritized remediation items and draft replacement copy.
- No production writes, publication, payment changes, private customer-data handling or credential collection.
- Delivery target: 3 business days after receiving the public URL and approved claim/source pack. This is a proposed service level, not an observed delivery record.
