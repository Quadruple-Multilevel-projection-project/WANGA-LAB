## 1. Canonical architecture
The proposal is a ten-stage functional loop: five forward-processing stages and five return stages. It is not proof that ten separate neural networks have been implemented. The four mathematical-logical weight layers attached to letter interpretations are separate; their original definitions and formulas must be recovered from research sources.

Keep separate:
- ten functional stages (5 forward + 5 return);
- four logical-mathematical weight layers;
- four sentence roles in the source-extraction method (subject, predicate, internal participant, relation/connective);
- evidence provenance (source, extraction, interpretation, engineering hypothesis).

Hard constraints are not soft scores. "Atomic/subatomic/metaphysical" refers to conceptual depth unless a physical mechanism is separately specified. Exact-text reconstruction and structural reconstruction are different tests.

## 2. Source boundary
The 79-page working PDF documents structured extraction from selected *Etz Chaim* passages and 28 units (`S-08-001…S-08-028`) concerning *Guide for the Perplexed* I:8. It preserves original sentences and sentence roles/relations, and explicitly defers a second logic until the first extraction is complete.

It does not establish the four weight formulas, complete 231-gate runtime, all 3D coordinates, full fractal-dimension computation, invertible text/mathematical representation, or reproducible LFA/CSR/EDI benchmark results.

## 7. Investor/product circuit
**Logic research → explicit specification → testable task-specific logic package → customer workflow → measured outcome → versioned improvement → research feedback.**

Potential packages may target finance/insurance, cross-border compliance, trade/supply chains, government procedure consistency, enterprise knowledge, or geopolitical analysis support. These are use-case hypotheses. Do not claim validated demand, zero crashes, perfect reconstruction, technical superiority, or reliable geopolitical prediction without data.

The product is the packaged logic unit with a task contract, rules, constraints, source lineage, versions, acceptance tests, and maintenance—not the page count alone.

## 8. Evidence and publication rules
