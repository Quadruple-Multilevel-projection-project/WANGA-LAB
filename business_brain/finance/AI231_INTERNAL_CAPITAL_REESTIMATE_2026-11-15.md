## Phased release plan
1. **Phase A — 0–12 months: USD 7M–9M.** Build and verify the software/logic processor prototype, secure architecture and evidence system, prove the first commercial offer, and run photonic/robotics feasibility work only through bounded external tests.
2. **Phase B — 12–24 months: USD 8M–10M.** Expand verified software, run selected TFLN/photonic test-chip iterations, build limited robotics demonstrators, and scale paid pilots if retention and gross margin support it.
3. **Phase C — 24–36 months: USD 7M–11M.** Integrate successful subsystems, complete independent validation, expand commercial delivery, and decide whether to fund a larger hardware program.

Do not release later phases automatically. Each requires technical evidence, buyer evidence, cost-to-complete and a fresh go/no-go decision.

## What is excluded
- Construction or ownership of a semiconductor fabrication plant.
- Advanced-node ASIC/SoC tape-out at 7nm/5nm/3nm as a committed deliverable.
- Mass production of robots or synthetic-organism hardware.
- Clinical trials, regulated medical-device development or human implantation.
- Large-scale frontier-model training or hyperscale data-centre construction.
- Any speculative valuation of Logic Coin or IP booked as cash.

If any excluded item becomes a real requirement, prepare a separate capital case; it can materially exceed this estimate.
