## 2026-10-08 — Consolidated research conclusions

### Future-field premise

The research does not claim to invent or control the future. The future is treated as a field in which new meanings, requirements, constraints, and engineering problems emerge. We can influence conditions, but we do not own the resulting meanings or outcomes. Therefore the operational objective is future-readiness rather than future invention.

### Logic Mine asset definition

The asset is not only historical logic extraction. It is a continuously upgraded engineering capability:

SOURCE → EXTRACTION → RECONSTRUCTION → LOGIC IDENTIFICATION → RELATIONS / DERIVATIONS → BOUNDARIES → CONFIGURATION → DRIFT FORENSICS → VERIFICATION → ADVANCED LOGIC STRUCTURE

A logic structure may retain value even when its current application is unknown. Application, technology, market demand, and architecture are dynamic variables; the preserved and tested structure is reusable research material.

### Engineering field hypothesis

The prospective field is not defined by a new kind of human cognition. It is defined by applying familiar engineering activities—abstraction, decomposition, design, construction, testing, debugging, verification, and optimization—to logical structures as engineering objects.

Potential engineering questions include: what a logical structure is; where it came from; what can be derived from it; its boundaries; its drift under context change; which domains it fits; how it composes with other structures; how it is verified; and how more advanced structures can be constructed from it.

This is a field-of-action hypothesis to be demonstrated through repeatable engineering work, not a claim that a mature academic or industrial discipline already exists under this name.

### Source-selection rule

Do not assume that every important text contains an extractable logic. A source is a Logic Mine candidate when a continuous logical structure can be demonstrated. Meaning, importance, literary value, or formal-looking language alone are insufficient. Historical, legal, philosophical, mathematical, or other sources are evaluated individually.

### Market/time rule

The current objective is to build what is useful now while preparing for capabilities that may become necessary as autonomous systems become more complex. Market direction and market speed are separate variables. A change in market direction should change the relevant configuration or logic layer, not invalidate the entire research program.

### Evidence discipline

Future claims remain hypotheses until supported by evidence. Current practical demonstrations are recorded separately from future scenarios. The research must continuously distinguish observation from assumption, evidence from inference, and NOT FOUND IN SEARCH from non-existence.

### Operational loop

OBSERVATION → ASSUMPTION → EVIDENCE → GAP → ARCHITECTURE CHANGE → TEST → NEW OBSERVATION

The purpose of the loop is cumulative adaptation: each cycle should increase the repository's evidence, tested structures, configuration knowledge, and ability to identify the next relevant logic layer.
