## Observation schema

Each observation must include:
- `observation_id`
- `hypothesis_id`
- `observed_at`
- `source_uri` and exact locator
- `literal_excerpt_or_summary`
- `source_quality`
- `measurement_window`
- `numerator`, `denominator`, and exclusions where applicable
- `supports / contradicts / neutral`
- `review_status`
- `reviewer`
- `next_review_date`

## Update rules

1. Do not call a hypothesis a fact because a milestone date has arrived.
2. Record disconfirming observations as well as supportive ones.
3. Keep missing evidence distinct from evidence of absence.
4. Do not use “exponential drift,” “90% friction,” “collapse,” or “complete transition” unless operational definitions and measured evidence are supplied.
5. Review at least annually when data is available; revise or retire hypotheses that fail their stated tests.
