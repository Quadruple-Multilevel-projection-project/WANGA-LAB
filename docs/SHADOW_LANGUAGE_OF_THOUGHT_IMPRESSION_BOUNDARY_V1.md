# Shadow Language of Thought — Impression Capture Boundary

**Status:** RESEARCH PROPOSAL · NOT YET FORMALIZED OR VERIFIED  
**Scope:** Four-impression capture network; planned 175 × 14 = 2,450 logical units.

## Core boundary

This system records and compares **impressions (reshimot)**. It does not photograph, capture, instantiate, or claim to understand the intellect itself.

The project separates:
- **Intellect / act of understanding:** in the human account guiding this research, the apprehending principle; not reducible to a record of impressions.
- **Logical thought-content:** abstract content and relations that can be defined, distinguished, checked for consistency, compared with reality, and tested through inference.
- **Impressions:** traces or records from which a machine can reconstruct selected logical moves.
- **Shadow language:** a machine-readable language of differences, contrasts, boundaries, relations, and transformations among impressions. It represents aspects of thought; it is not thought itself.

“Shadow language” is a project term and research proposal, not an established scientific construct.

## Human–machine asymmetry

| Human account | Machine system |
|---|---|
| The intellect is the principle that apprehends and retains understanding. | No intellect is presumed or claimed. |
| Impressions serve the intellect during apprehension; the intellect need not preserve every original trace after understanding. | The system must preserve impressions and provenance because reconstruction depends on recorded traces. |
| The act of understanding is not identical to its logical description. | The system operates on descriptions, differences, and relations among impressions. |
| The intellect is not inferred merely from an external record. | Successful replay of a logical move is not evidence of a machine intellect. |

This is the project's conceptual distinction, not an empirical proof of a complete theory of human cognition or machine consciousness.

## Five matters are not the four impression channels

The user identifies **five distinct matters** involved in abstract thought, which cannot be collapsed into a verbal description and which, in the human account, are inseparable from living understanding. Their exact definitions and relations have not yet been supplied as a source-grounded formal enumeration.

Therefore:
- preserve the count “five” as a research anchor;
- do not invent names, definitions, order, or relations;
- represent them as `FIVE_MATTERS_UNSPECIFIED` pending source text and review;
- do not equate them with the four machine-side impression channels.

The four channels are a capture scheme. The five matters belong to the proposed account of abstract thought. They remain separate until a formal mapping is supplied.

## Four impression channels

1. **I1 — External / first-order:** source word, wording, quotation, literal definition, and locator.
2. **I2 — External / second-order:** explicit relations among terms, definitions, propositions, and contexts.
3. **I3 — Internal / cognitive representation:** observable machine-side processing traces and transformations. “Internal” means internal to the computational representation, not private subjective experience.
4. **I4 — Internal / ontological representation:** the model's explicit representation of a referent, category, differentia, property, accident, or declared distinction. It is a representation, not the entity itself or proof of apprehension.

## Difference as an operative primitive

Candidate relation types for the shadow-language graph:

- `DISTINGUISHES`
- `CONTRASTS_WITH`
- `PRESUPPOSES`
- `DEPENDS_ON`
- `COMPOSES_WITH`
- `CONTRADICTS`
- `INVARIANT_UNDER`
- `LOST_IN_TRANSFORMATION`
- `RECONSTRUCTED_FROM`
- `UNRESOLVED_AGAINST`

Every relation must retain provenance, exact source span/data locator, method and version, and validation state. Difference is not automatically contradiction; relation types must remain distinct.

## What can be captured and tested

The network can produce inspectable evidence about:
- which impressions were captured;
- which distinctions were preserved or lost;
- which logical transitions can be replayed deterministically;
- which relations are source-supported, inferred, disputed, or unresolved;
- how alternate representations affect reconstruction;
- whether a proposed logical move survives counterexamples and independent review.

Those records alone cannot demonstrate that the machine has a human-like intellect, subjective experience, that the human intellect was captured, or that replay equals living understanding.

## Capture experiment for the 2,450-unit matrix

For each planned chapter-term unit:
1. preserve the original source and SHA-256;
2. capture available impressions in I1–I4;
3. record distinctions and transformations in the shadow-language graph;
4. retain missing/conflicting impressions as explicit null/conflict records;
5. replay the recorded logical move deterministically;
6. compare replay with source-grounded expected distinctions;
7. save a visual snapshot of the representation graph, provenance, and unresolved edges;
8. report preservation, loss, reconstruction, and error counts separately.

A visual snapshot is a photograph of the **recorded representation and its relations**, not of the intellect.

The arithmetic 175 × 14 = 2,450 defines a planned matrix; it does not establish that 2,450 Excel files have been located or ingested.

## Evidence labels

Use explicit statuses only:
`SOURCE_VERIFIED`, `EXTRACTED`, `MAPPED_CANDIDATE`, `REPLAYED_DETERMINISTICALLY`, `INDEPENDENTLY_CHECKED`, `CONFLICT`, `MISSING`, `NOT_YET_VERIFIED`.

Do not introduce automatic `UNDERSTOOD` or `INTELLECT_CAPTURED` statuses.
