## Definition

A condition intended to remain true when validated logical fragments are composed at a higher scale, such as typed interface compatibility, stable parent IDs, source lineage, and preserved constraints.

## Scope and limitations

An invariant must be stated as an executable predicate with positive and negative tests. Similar shapes, shared terms, or successful serialization are not semantic proof.
