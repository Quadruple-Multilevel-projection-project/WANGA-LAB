## Publication rule

Each published article needs actual source passages, precise references, alternative readings, a worked example and a counterexample, typed relationships, and test results when it claims implementation or performance. A page definition is not a substitute for finished article content.

## Mathematical integrity guard

- 231 = C(22,2), unordered pairs without self-pairs.
- 462 = 22×21, ordered pairs without repetition.
- 1,540 = C(22,3), unordered sets of three distinct letters.
- 9,240 = 22×21×20, ordered triples of distinct letters.
- 10,648 = 22³, ordered triples when repetition is allowed.
- Keep the 221/231 textual variation visible and record edition/witness.
- The original four weight-layer formulas remain to be recovered, not guessed.
- Lie algebra, E8, Tonnetz, 10 spherical degrees of freedom and fractal point clouds remain hypotheses until mappings, operators, invariants and tests are specified.
