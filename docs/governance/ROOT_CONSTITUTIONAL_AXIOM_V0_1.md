## 0. Authority and scope

This document records the architect's correction that the primary axiom is the constitution: it is prior to, and has authority over, every system law. It is not merely a design principle, engineering preference, policy recommendation, or ordinary rule.

The semantic-topology domain is not to be reduced to a physical geometry, a drawing, or a metaphor. The system is a thought/logic architecture whose abstract semantic relations are represented and operated through letters and their source-governed relations. Circles, lines, dimensions, tesseracts and similar terms may occur in source descriptions or explanatory projections; they must not be mistaken for physical components or treated as the ontology of the processor.

Important source-control limitation: this file records the constitutional status and operational consequences established in the current instruction. It does not claim to reproduce the complete canonical wording of AXIOM-001. The architect's authoritative full text must be captured verbatim from its designated source before the axiom can be marked sealed or source-verified. No agent may fill in missing wording by inference.

## 1. Constitutional precedence

Authority order:
1. AXIOM-001 — Primary Constitutional Axiom / Constitution.
2. Root laws explicitly derived from AXIOM-001 and accepted by the human architect.
3. Versioned contracts and specifications that implement those laws.
4. Semantic representations: letters, letter relations, combinations, gates, sparks, pipeline symbols, memory states and their relations.
5. Runtime mechanisms, agents, models, tools, data stores, diagrams and execution environments.

Lower levels cannot amend, reinterpret, suspend, override or grant exceptions to a higher level. A model consensus, majority vote, successful computation, business objective, performance gain, visual resemblance, or external research result cannot supersede the Constitution.

## 2. Mandatory pre-action constitutional gate

Before any agent, model, pipeline, plugin, tool or human-operated automation performs a core semantic/logical operation, it must:
- identify the applicable constitutional clause and derived law;
- identify the exact source/version and provenance;
- show that the requested operation is permitted by that law;
- preserve the distinction between source statement, interpretation, hypothesis and implementation;
- return an auditable decision receipt.

If the clause, source, derivation, permission or interpretation is missing or contradictory, the operation must ABSTAIN or be BLOCKED. Do not invent a rule, complete missing axiom text, or silently continue.

Documentation-only work may record this directive and identify gaps. It must not claim that the constitutional axiom has been fully registered, verified or sealed.

## 3. Root semantic boundary

The semantic topology is a governing semantic/logical structure, not a physical layout. Its meaning and operation must be defined by the authoritative source and the Constitution.

- Letters are not merely display characters.
- Letter combinations are not merely numeric indexes.
- Gates are not authorized merely because a pair can be enumerated.
- A relation is not valid merely because a graph edge can be drawn.
- A transition is not permitted merely because an AI model predicts it.
- A diagram is a projection of an understood relation; it is not proof of the relation.
- No physical, quantum, crystallographic or dimensional interpretation may be inferred from a semantic representation without separate evidence and an explicitly approved mapping.

The 22 letters, 231 gates, 288 sparks, pipeline symbols, memory snapshots and any agent counts remain distinct registries. Their identities, relationships and cardinalities must not be conflated.

## 4. Separation of constitution, logic and business

These domains are not interchangeable:
- Constitution: establishes the highest authority and the conditions under which laws may exist.
- Logic: defines and evaluates relations, distinctions, combinations, inferences and valid transitions under the Constitution.
- Business: governs operational, economic, resource, commercial and execution concerns within its own authorized scope.

Business utility, cost, market demand, funding, throughput or convenience cannot modify logical validity or constitutional authority. Logical validity cannot be replaced by commercial preference. A business workflow cannot grant an agent semantic authority.

## 7. Constitutional Enforcement Layer — implementation contract

### 7.1 Decision rule
- The gate runs before dispatch, tool invocation, state mutation, synthesis that changes canonical state, LOCK, release or external side effects.
- It is fail-closed: missing, malformed, contradictory or unverified evidence returns BLOCKED.
- Documentation-only recording may return PASS_FOR_REVIEW, which is not execution permission.
- Only an explicit PASS permits dispatch. PASS_FOR_REVIEW, CANDIDATE, ABSTAIN, BLOCKED and HUMAN_REVIEW never permit dispatch.
- A model's own assertion that the source is verified is not proof. The runtime must validate the canonical source reference and approval receipt against trusted registries.

### 7.2 Minimum core-operation checks
A core task must provide:
1. constitutional_axiom_id exactly AXIOM-001;
2. canonical constitution_source_ref and constitution_version;
3. independently verified canonical source status;
4. at least one approved derived_law_ref;
5. requested_operation included in allowed_operations;
6. source_refs with exact locators;
7. no unresolved constitutional conflicts;
8. explicit architect_approval_receipt for the operation's authorization scope.

### 7.3 Reference implementation
A fail-closed reference evaluator is provided at `governance/constitutional_gate.py`, with unit tests at `tests/test_constitutional_gate.py`. It emits a SHA-256 decision receipt. It is a policy evaluator only: it does not intercept agents, tools, providers or runtime dispatch by itself.

### 7.4 Integration requirements
The orchestrator must call the evaluator before dispatch and enforce `may_dispatch(decision) == true`. All provider adapters, plugins, edge workers and state-write paths must route through the same gate. Direct bypass paths must be removed or denied. Gate failures must be logged with the receipt and escalated without retrying through an alternate route.
