# First Revenue Product — Wix Agency Operations Copilot

**Status: Hypothesis only. No paying demand has been demonstrated yet.**

## Buyer
Small Wix Studio agencies and freelancers managing multiple client sites.

## Proposed outcome
Reduce recurring manual website operations work and give agencies clear, prioritized, client-ready maintenance and improvement work orders.

## Smallest sellable offer
A human-approved 2–4 week paid pilot for a limited number of authorized client sites:
1. Baseline site review.
2. Prioritized findings across maintenance, content, SEO and lead flows.
3. Drafted work orders and client-ready summaries.
4. Completion tracking and measured time saved.

No autonomous production edits in the first pilot. The buyer must authorize access to each site.

## Why this is technically plausible
Wix documents APIs for site data, CMS, CRM, business management, SEO/site settings, and account/site management. Wix's Sites APIs allow authorized account users, resellers, agencies and third-party developers to retrieve site details and perform supported site actions. Actual access depends on OAuth, scopes, account permissions and API support; each workflow must be tested.

Sources:
- https://dev.wix.com/docs/overview/platform-overview/about-wix-apis
- https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/account-level/sites/introduction
- https://dev.wix.com/docs/go-headless/project-management/add-apps-to-a-project

Wix states that its developer platform allows apps to be distributed through the Wix App Market. This is a potential channel, not evidence that this product will be accepted or discovered there:
- https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-for-developers

## Market signal — not proof of demand
A 2026 Dataprovider report estimates approximately 4.73 million Wix-powered websites. This is a broad installed-base signal, not a count of agencies, addressable buyers or willingness to pay:
- https://www.dataprovider.com/research/pdf/dataprovider-cms-market-share.pdf

Published website-maintenance plans across the market indicate existing paid spend on maintenance, but available examples are mainly WordPress and are not proof of Wix-agency demand:
- https://dardo.studio/en/blog/website-maintenance-cost/

## Validation before build
- 30 prospect list; 10 qualified buyer conversations.
- Record actual workflow, time spent, pain frequency, current tools, budget and buying authority.
- Test a specific paid pilot offer and price.
- Require at least one paid pilot/deposit or signed purchase commitment before expanding engineering.
- Compare alternatives and calculate delivery cost, gross margin and customer acquisition effort.

## Kill criteria
Stop or change the product if buyers do not confirm recurring costly pain, incumbents solve it adequately, no one will pay for the pilot, or Wix permissions make delivery impractical.

## Commercial rule
We sell a measurable business outcome, not an AI agent. The agent is an internal production method; the customer buys time saved, fewer missed issues and a better managed client portfolio.
