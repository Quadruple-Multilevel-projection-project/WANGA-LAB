## Definition

A proposed recursively composable logical structure in which validated primitives and relations form substructures that can be composed into higher-level structures while preserving constraints, provenance and counterexample results.

## Scope and limitations

Self-similarity or geometric fractality alone does not establish logical validity. Grammar, composition operators and invariant-preservation conditions remain to be formalized.
