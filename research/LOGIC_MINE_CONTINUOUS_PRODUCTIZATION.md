# Logic Mine — Continuous Logic Extraction & Future Productization

## Status

**PLANNED / RESEARCH DIRECTION**

This document defines the working commercial-research model for WANGA-LAB.

WANGA-LAB is not a finite archive of extracted formal logic. The research process is a **continuous logic-mining operation**: source material, existing computational systems, historical logic, language structures, and future capability gaps remain inputs to an ongoing process of extraction, reconstruction, invention, formalization, testing, and verification.

## Core metaphor

WANGA-LAB is treated as a **mine of logic**.

The product is not the mine itself and not a static list of logical rules. The mine is the continuously operated research capability that produces new logic assets.

As a physical mine can produce copper, silver, gold, or gemstones, the intended research-commercial pipeline produces **logic assets** of different depth, structure, applicability, and verification status.

The analogy is structural, not a claim about commodity economics.

## Future product market

The intended market is a future agent-centric software layer in which agents can discover, request, acquire, configure, and execute specialized logic capabilities.

The target products are therefore **shelf-ready logic capabilities for a future market**, not merely a sequence of academic logic exercises.

Possible product forms include:

- reusable logic packages;
- agent-specific logic configurations;
- bundles of complementary logic capabilities;
- task/workflow shells containing multiple logic packages;
- temporary rental or subscription access;
- licensed packages;
- customized configurations for a specific agent or runtime;
- future machine-readable packages discoverable by autonomous agents.

These are product hypotheses until commercially validated.

## Two clocks

The research uses two planning horizons:

- **24–36 months:** active forward-looking development window.
- **5–10 years:** strategic horizon for anticipating the next agent/network/software layer.

The window moves forward continuously. It is not a fixed prediction date.

## Research-to-market distinction

The same pipeline serves two functions:

1. **Research:** discover and develop logic that does not yet have a runnable implementation or suitable runtime.
2. **Commercialization:** package verified or appropriately classified logic capabilities for use when the required execution layer becomes available.

A logic asset may therefore exist as a defined configuration before current systems can execute it at its intended depth.

## Evidence discipline

No logic is promoted to VERIFIED merely because it is conceptually interesting.

Maintain explicit states:

**SOURCE · EXTRACTED · RECONSTRUCTED · FORMALIZED · DERIVED · MAPPED · TESTED · VERIFIED · HYPOTHETICAL · NOT_YET_VERIFIED · OPEN · BLOCKED**

The mine can contain hypotheses and unfinished seams, but product claims must preserve their evidence state.

## Strategic thesis

The intended durable asset is not dependence on a particular model, provider, or runtime.

The intended durable asset is the **capacity to continuously discover, develop, verify, and productize logic architectures above changing model and runtime layers**.

When underlying models change, the mine continues.

When runtimes change, configurations can be remapped.

When new agent capabilities create new requirements, those requirements become new research seams.

## Boundary

This document does not claim:

- that the future agent market already exists at scale;
- that a particular logic package will retain commercial value;
- that WANGA-LAB has already demonstrated a market;
- that every proposed logic architecture is novel;
- that future automation will eliminate all other forms of work.

Those are empirical or commercial questions to be tested.

The durable working proposition is narrower:

**Continuous logic mining → continuous logic productization → continuous adaptation to the next computational layer.**
