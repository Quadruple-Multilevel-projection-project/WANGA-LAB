## 4. Partnership economics
- Prefer non-exclusive, task-scoped collaboration with clear deliverables, acceptance criteria, IP ownership and data-export rights.
- Pay external partners only from documented operating cash or an explicitly approved funding instrument; no share, token or guaranteed-return promise is implied.
- Keep foundational IP, customer deliverables, partner contributions and internal Logic Credits as separate ledgers.
- Do not make a partner dependent on an unverified Logic Coin valuation or on a single cloud/model vendor.

## 5. Capital allocation — WIN WITHOUT DEPENDENCE

1. validate one narrow customer problem;

Accept only if the risk-adjusted net benefit exceeds the independent baseline and the project retains acceptable IP, data, pricing and operating control. No grant or investor is assumed or required.
