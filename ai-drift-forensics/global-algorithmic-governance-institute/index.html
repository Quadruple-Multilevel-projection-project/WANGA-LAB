What the institute is building

The institute layer defines research and institutional interfaces around technical evidence systems. It does not replace WANGA-LAB, and it does not itself make legal, regulatory, insurance, or certification determinations.

Drift Science

Taxonomy, baseline/delta measurement, model and configuration drift, agent/tool drift, dependency drift, and reproducibility.

Evidence & Forensics

Case objects, provenance, observation records, competing causal explanations, verification gates, and evidence-chain structures.

Standards Research

Terminology, measurement definitions, reference tests, conformance profiles, interoperability schemas, and mapping to existing standards.

Regulatory Interface Research

Technical evidence-to-requirement mapping for different supervisory environments without asserting regulatory authority.

Insurance / Risk Interface

Research into whether reproducible drift evidence can reduce uncertainty for monitoring, investigation, and risk-transfer analysis.

Global Research Network

Coordination between research nodes, verification workflows, technical standards work, and institutional interfaces.

Research boundary

This public layer is a research-program surface. It is not evidence of government recognition, court authority, insurer status, certification authority, or an adopted international standard.
