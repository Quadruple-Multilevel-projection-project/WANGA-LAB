AI231 Robotics Collaboration is the partner gateway for universities, photonics laboratories, semiconductor fabricators, robotics teams and industrial research groups exploring a language-based logical processor and its possible physical applications.

The purpose is to define measurable collaborations, test specific hypotheses and publish evidence. This portal does not claim that the complete AI231 processor or a robot based on it has already been built.

02 / Technical scope

What the research is trying to establish.

Core requirements

  • 231 deterministic logical gates, with each gate's semantics to be formally specified.
  • A declared 15,625-point topology whose dimensional construction and addressing remain unresolved.
  • An internal language pathway that preserves source-defined relations.
  • Logic-before-weights: no numerical weight matrix is assumed or introduced.

Candidate experimental platforms

  • TFLN / LNOI photonic integration and electro-optic control.
  • Programmable photonic meshes and polariton microcavities as candidate platforms.
  • Electronic/optical control and measurement interfaces.
  • Majorana-based protection only if operational topological-qubit functionality is independently established.
Important dimensional check: 5×5×5 equals 125, not 15,625. The 15,625-point requirement is preserved, but its dimensional/generative definition remains unresolved until the source specification is reconciled.
03 / First joint experiment

A small result that can be measured and reproduced.

The proposed minimum viable experiment is deliberately narrow: implement and measure a documented subset of source-defined language relations on a candidate photonic or polariton platform.

Define the logical subset

Record the source definition, allowed transformations, invariants and expected output before fabrication.

Map to a physical experiment

Specify the candidate substrate, optical paths/phases/polarization states, control signals and readout.

Measure and repeat

Capture raw outputs, repeatability, noise, fabrication tolerance and environmental sensitivity.

Independent review

Compare measured behavior with the source-defined relation set and publish positive, negative and inconclusive results.

Acceptance principle: success means measured physical outputs preserve the specified relations under documented conditions. A conventional Boolean demonstration or a photonic component demonstration alone does not establish AI231 gate realization.
04 / Partnership protocol

Clear scope. Traceable work. Defined rights.

This is a non-binding collaboration framework for discussion. Each actual project requires a separate written agreement.

Operating clauses

Purpose & deliverables

Each work package names the research question, milestones, deliverables and objective acceptance criteria.

Roles & resources

Each party records the people, equipment, facilities, funding and time it is actually committing.

Background IP

Pre-existing IP stays with its owner. Access is granted only under a written license or project agreement.

Foreground IP

Ownership, inventorship, licensing and commercialization of new results are defined in a separate agreement before work begins.

Data & confidentiality

Data access, retention, security, confidential information and laboratory safety are documented per project.

Publication & exit

Authorship, review periods, disclosure, termination and handling of shared materials are agreed in writing.

Evidence and governance

  • Every technical claim is labeled Verified, Assessment, Hypothesis or Unresolved.
  • Every experiment keeps source definitions, protocol versions, raw data and analysis provenance.
  • Changes to logical definitions or acceptance thresholds require recorded human approval.
  • No partner may imply endorsement, completed implementation or commercial readiness without evidence and authorization.
  • Safety, export-control, privacy and regulatory obligations are assessed by the responsible parties.
Legal boundary: this page creates no binding contract, equity, exclusivity, investment commitment, IP assignment, license or commercial promise. A signed agreement between the relevant parties is required for any such commitment.
05 / Evidence status

What is established — and what is not.

Verified component capability

TFLN / LNOI

High-speed electro-optic modulation and integrated photonic components are demonstrated. AI231-specific logical behavior is not thereby verified.

Verified component capability

Polariton experiments

Simple temporal Boolean operations have been demonstrated in polariton systems. This does not establish language-relational gates.

Not yet verified

Majorana qubit

Operational topological-qubit functionality is not treated as established for this program.

Unresolved

231 gate mapping

The physical implementation and acceptance test for each source-defined gate remain open.

Unresolved

15,625-point lattice

No full physical implementation is claimed; dimensional definition and hardware addressing remain open.

Proposed research

Integrated processor

A staged engineering hypothesis requiring simulation, laboratory evidence, replication and independent review.
