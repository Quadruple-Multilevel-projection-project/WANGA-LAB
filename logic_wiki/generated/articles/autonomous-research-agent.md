## Definition

A bounded work unit with explicit inputs, outputs, evidence requirements and stop conditions, such as source collection, extraction, composition, adversarial review, benchmarking or wiki curation.

## Scope and limitations

A registered role is not itself an autonomous AI capability. Execution is claimed only when a run produces a report; access gaps, failures and blocked work must be recorded.
