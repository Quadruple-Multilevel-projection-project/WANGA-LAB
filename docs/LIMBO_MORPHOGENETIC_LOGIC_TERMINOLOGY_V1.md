# Limbo / Morphogenetic Logic — Working Terminology

**Status:** WORKING_GLOSSARY · INTERPRETIVE · NOT_YET_VERIFIED  
**Purpose:** Preserve the project's own vocabulary without silently translating it into formal logic, statistics, biology, or economics. This is a terminology aid, not a claim that the concepts have already been operationally validated.

## Operating stance

Start from **NOT_UNDERSTOOD_YET**. Do not assume that a term has been fully understood because it resembles a known technical term. Preserve the original wording and context, and distinguish the project's intended meaning from any implementation proposal.

The system's outer program is intended to map, alongside the phenomenon called *Limbo*, the development, failure, expansion, and deployment of logical structures. This outer program is not identical to the logic structures it may later help compose.

## Working terms

### Limbo
The project's name for a condition in which a structure collapses under a convergence of statistical/probabilistic forces, exposing a structural deficiency. Limbo is not merely a score for model error, a synonym for uncertainty, or a conventional anomaly detector. Its precise operational indicators remain to be specified from project examples and evidence.

### Collapse point
A located event or configuration at which the current structure no longer holds together under the conditions being mapped. A recorded failure is evidence that a failure occurred in a specified context; it does not by itself prove a universal limit of statistics or prove the correctness of a replacement structure.

### Expanded mapping
The act of placing a collapse point into a larger structure so that previously inaccessible relations, dependencies, or meanings may become visible. The larger mapping is a candidate representation and must retain links to the original observation and the reasoning used to extend it.

### Keystone
A candidate element whose role may be structurally central in a particular mapping. Removal and substitution tests can provide evidence about that role, but a test result must not automatically promote the element to a verified axiom. Record the target structure, transformation, observed outcome, alternative explanations, and scope.

### Morphogenetic logic
The project's working name for the study of how logical structures form, change, fail, and reorganize into broader structures. “Morphogenetic” is not proof that the system is a biological organism or that biological mechanisms have been empirically demonstrated.

### Convergence signal
A source-backed observation that may indicate a transition, constraint, adoption, or structural pressure relevant to the project's model. Signals are observations to assess, not proof of a predicted future. Record source, date, locator, context, alternative readings, confidence basis, and verification status.

### Logic orchestration
The selection and arrangement of logical structures in response to the mapped state at a given time. The orchestration policy is not yet fully specified; do not invent rules for selecting a logic package until grounded in project examples, contracts, and tests.

### Outer program / deployment wrapper
The long-horizon program that collects observations, maps Limbo, compares evolving structures with reality, tracks convergence signals, and determines what should be developed or deployed next. It must remain distinct from the logical structures built inside it.

### Logical meaning (non-formal)
A project term for meaning and structural relations that are not exhausted by formal validity or symbolic derivation. Do not force every such relation into first-order logic at ingestion time. Preserve it as a source-linked candidate until its structure and permissible transformations are clearer.

## Evidence discipline

Every record must distinguish:
- **SOURCE:** where the material was observed.
- **LITERAL EXCERPT:** what the source actually says.
- **OBSERVATION:** what was directly seen or measured.
- **INTERPRETATION:** a reading of that observation.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** a proposed explanation or expected relation.
- **IMPLEMENTATION:** code or configuration that encodes specified behavior.
- **TEST RESULT:** the exact check run and its actual result.
- **VERIFICATION:** a claim limited to the scope of the evidence that supports it.

Do not label a convergence signal, keystone, axiom, market shift, or collapse VERIFIED solely because it fits the current narrative. Keep competing readings and negative cases visible.

## Do-not-collapse distinctions

1. **Limbo is not just a numeric metric.** Metrics may be introduced later as instruments, but they do not define the concept by themselves.
2. **A structural interpretation is not a measured fact.** Keep the source observation and interpretation separately addressable.
3. **A failed structure does not automatically validate its replacement.** The expanded mapping needs its own evidence and counter-tests.
4. **A weight is not a missing relation.** Do not use numeric weights, visual similarity, or fractal appearance to invent semantic connections that have not been established.
5. **Formal logic is a possible tool, not the complete ontology.** Use formalization where it preserves the relation being described; retain unresolved meaning without pretending it has been formalized.
6. **Biological language is currently an analogy/model label.** Do not infer biological implementation, immune mechanisms, or empirical biological validation from the terminology alone.
7. **Forecast windows are hypotheses.** The 2026–2040 horizon organizes observation; it does not establish that a predicted transition is inevitable or already verified.

## Required record for a proposed signal or collapse point

Minimum fields:
- stable ID and version
- source URI and exact locator
- observed date and source publication date, if available
- literal excerpt or a privacy-safe summary
- context and affected structure
- proposed Limbo/collapse interpretation
- expanded mapping candidate and its parent links
- alternative reading / counterexample
- scope and non-applicability conditions
- tests run and actual outcomes
- epistemic status: OBSERVED, INTERPRETATION, HYPOTHESIS, IMPLEMENTED, TESTED, or VERIFIED
- reviewer and next action

Missing evidence must be represented as missing, not inferred.

## Open questions — deliberately unresolved

- What observable conditions distinguish a Limbo collapse point from an ordinary local failure?
- How is a larger structure selected without assuming the desired conclusion?
- Which transformations preserve the intended logical meaning?
- What evidence is sufficient to call an element a Keystone within a stated scope?
- How should the system choose which logic to orchestrate at a particular time?
- Which convergence signals are independent, and which are duplicate reports of the same event?
- What would count as a counterexample to the long-horizon transition hypothesis?

These questions are part of the work queue. Do not close them by adding arbitrary thresholds or definitions.
