# WANGA-LAB — Evidence, Logic Mining, Drift Forensics & Verification

WANGA-LAB is the canonical research and engineering repository for **AI evidence integrity, drift forensics, provenance reconstruction, verification, and continuous logic mining**.

The repository has two connected research directions:

1. **Present operational capability:** establish what happened in AI systems, preserve evidence, reconstruct behavior, detect drift, and independently verify findings.
2. **Future-facing engineering capability:** identify, extract, formalize, configure, test, and evolve logical structures that may become useful as AI systems and their engineering requirements change.

The second direction does **not** claim to predict or control future outcomes. The future is treated as a field in which new meanings, requirements, constraints, and engineering problems emerge. The research objective is therefore **future-readiness, not future invention**.

## WANGA and Rational Logic

WANGA is treated in this research as a distinct computing architecture, not simply as a wrapper around a language model.

Within WANGA:

**Rational Logic = the canonical logic and reasoning layer**

This is a statement about system architecture. It does not claim separate physical hardware. The public repository documents the relationship between the system and its logic layer; deeper formal details and proprietary implementation remain outside the public disclosure boundary.

## Core distinction

WANGA-LAB is both an **independent evidence layer around AI systems** and a research environment for engineering logical structures.

For forensic work:

**Evidence Protocol → Drift Artifact → Verification Chain → Governance Interface**

For logic research:

**Source → Extraction → Reconstruction → Logic Identification → Relations / Derivations → Boundaries → Configuration → Drift Forensics → Verification → Advanced Logic Structure**

These are connected but distinct evidence paths. Logic is not assumed merely because a source is important, meaningful, historical, philosophical, or formally written. A source becomes a Logic Mine candidate when a continuous logical structure can be demonstrated.

## Service scope

WANGA-LAB / Drift Forensics is intended to provide independent technical evidence, AI-drift analysis, model-behavior analysis, provenance reconstruction, and systemic-exposure assessment to insurance companies and institutional risk holders with material exposure to banks and other critical financial infrastructure.

The service is an evidence and forensic-analysis layer. It does not itself provide insurance coverage, underwriting, a financial guarantee, solvency assurance, or a regulated insurance product. Commercial and regulatory classification must be reviewed for the applicable jurisdiction before customer use.

## Evidence and verification

A representative verification chain is:

**Canonical Representation → SHA-256 Integrity → Timestamp Evidence → Deterministic Replay → Comparison → Verification Result**

The exact mechanism set depends on the case. Hashes, timestamps, anchors, signatures, replay and verification answer different evidentiary questions and must not be treated as interchangeable.

External timestamping or anchoring is not treated as complete until actual proof is received and checked.

## Drift forensics

The central research sequence is:

**Baseline → Observation → Drift Detection → Evidence Preservation → Reconstruction → Causal / Dependency Analysis → Attribution → Risk Quantification → Intervention → Verification**

The evaluation chain is:

**Model → Answer → Evaluation → Correction → Criterion Change → Re-evaluation**

Research distinguishes point error from changes in definition, premise, inference, criteria, question fidelity, evidence/source fidelity, terminology, and response trajectory.

## Continuous Logic Mine

WANGA-LAB treats logic as a reusable research asset, not merely as a historical archive.

The asset includes more than extraction. It includes knowledge of a structure's **relations, derivations, boundaries, domain fit, configuration, drift behavior, verification state, and possible construction of more advanced structures**.

A logical structure does not need an immediate application to justify preservation as research material. Technology, markets, applications, architectures, and demand are dynamic variables; the underlying structure can be revisited and reconfigured when conditions change.

The working loop is:

**Continuous Logic Mining → Logic Architecture → Formalization / Configuration → Verification → Productization → Agent / Runtime Package → Deployment / Licensing / Rental → Operational Evidence → New Mining**

The commercial market and specific product demand remain hypotheses until validated by evidence.

## Prospective Logic Engineering field

The research hypothesis is that increasing system autonomy may move some engineering work toward the design, configuration, evolution, and verification of logical structures and reasoning architectures.

This does not claim that existing engineers disappear or that a mature academic discipline already exists under this name. The proposed field applies familiar engineering activities—abstraction, decomposition, design, construction, testing, debugging, verification, and optimization—to logical structures as engineering objects.

Potential engineering questions include:

- What is the structure and provenance of a logical system?
- What relations and derivations does it contain?
- Where are its boundaries?
- How does it drift when context changes?
- Which domains are appropriate for it?
- How can it be composed with other structures?
- How can it be configured for a concrete agent or task?
- How can its behavior be independently verified?
- What more advanced structures can be constructed from it?

This remains a **research hypothesis to be demonstrated through repeatable engineering and market evidence**, not a claim of an already-established profession or industry.

## Future and market discipline

The program is future-facing without being future-dependent.

The working principle is:

**Build what is useful now → observe what the future actually produces → identify the new requirement → adapt the relevant logic/configuration layer → test → verify.**

Market direction and market speed are separate variables. A change in market direction should trigger configuration or logic-layer adjustment rather than invalidate the entire research program.

The market is a test of utility, not a substitute for evidence.

## Evidence discipline

The repository distinguishes:

**OBSERVATION ≠ ASSUMPTION ≠ EVIDENCE ≠ INFERENCE ≠ HYPOTHESIS**

Evidence states remain explicit:

**BUILT · SPECIFIED · PROTOTYPED · TESTED · VERIFIED · PLANNED · HYPOTHETICAL**

A search failure is not evidence of non-existence. A future scenario is not a current fact. A research hypothesis is not promoted to VERIFIED without corresponding evidence.

## Legal and governance boundary

Software and cryptographic evidence mechanisms do not automatically establish legal admissibility, regulatory status, underwriting authority, or contractual enforceability.

WANGA-LAB therefore keeps technical evidence, governance research, legal review, institutional authority, and protected intellectual property as distinct layers.

## Public / protected model

The public research layer documents:

**methodology · architecture · provenance · drift analysis · verification interfaces · reproducible evidence artifacts · Logic Mine research framework**

The protected layer may retain:

**implementation details · algorithms · mechanisms · proprietary technical material**

This separation is intentional. The public system documents the evidence and research capability without publishing protected reasoning implementation.
